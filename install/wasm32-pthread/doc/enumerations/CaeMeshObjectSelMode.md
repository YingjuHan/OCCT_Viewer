# Enumeration: CaeMeshObjectSelMode

CaeMeshObjectSelMode represents selection mode of [InteractiveCaeMesh](../classes/InteractiveCaeMesh.md), from `InteractiveCaeMeshSelMode`.
It is defined as a bitmask to support several active selection mode simultaneously in the viewer.

## Enumeration Members

### Elements

> **Elements**: `2`

Selection of mesh elements.

***

### Nodes

> **Nodes**: `4`

Selection of mesh nodes.

***

### None

> **None**: `0`

Disabled selection.

***

### Presentation

> **Presentation**: `1`

Selection of mesh presentation.
